## Known limitations

1. Version horizon: the source is the IORT edition revised 15 February 2024. It consolidates the Code de commerce as amended up to at least loi n° 2019-47 du 29 mai 2019 (the latest amendment the text cites). Amendments adopted after that date are absent. The official JORT archive at https://www.jort.tn/ is where they would be verified; that reconciliation is not part of this corpus.
2. Local-only source: the 2024 edition is not served by the publisher's public URL (that URL still returns the 2014 edition), so the pipeline cannot download it. The raw PDF must be present in data/raw/ and is verified against the recorded SHA-256. Because the raw PDF is gitignored, a fresh clone can only rebuild the corpus if the file is supplied again.
3. Repealed articles are absent from the published consolidated text (the table of contents marks them '(abrogés)', and the printed notice lists the range). The corpus therefore does not contain the repealed wording either.
4. Article 465 prints its header and an amendment note in the 2024 edition but no article text, while the table of contents marks it '(article 465 abrogé)'. It is recorded with status 'repealed' and empty text, keeping the printed note. The source is internally inconsistent here; this is not resolved against the JORT.
5. Amendment provenance is what the edition prints inline, for example '(Modifié par la loi n° 96-28 du 3 avril 1996)'. It is reproduced faithfully but not independently verified against the JORT.
6. Footnotes (rectificatifs and editorial notes printed at page bottoms) are kept in annotations.jsonl, not merged into article text, because they are not part of the enacted article.
7. The Arabic version of the same code is not machine-readable: its PDF has a broken ToUnicode map and extracts as glyph names, not Arabic text. See excluded_sources in manifest.json.
8. Article text is re-flowed for readability: hard line breaks are joined, hyphenated word wraps are repaired, and enumerated items keep their own line. Wording, punctuation and article numbering are unchanged, but the line breaks do not match the printed page.
9. Heading titles are reproduced as published, including upper-case typography, and renumbered words: the published 'LIVRE PREMIER' is recorded as 'Livre I' so book labels sort consistently.
