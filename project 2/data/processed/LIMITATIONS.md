## Known limitations

1. Version horizon: the source consolidates amendments up to loi n° 2010-39 du 26 juillet 2010 (IORT edition dated 2014-02-15). Amendments adopted after that date are absent. The official JORT archive at https://www.jort.tn/ is where they would be verified; that reconciliation is not part of v1.
2. Repealed articles are absent from the published consolidated text (the table of contents marks them '(abrogés)'). The corpus therefore does not contain the repealed wording either.
3. Amendment provenance is what the edition prints inline, for example '(Modifié par la loi n° 96-28 du 3 avril 1996)'. It is reproduced faithfully but not independently verified against the JORT.
4. Footnotes (rectificatifs and editorial notes printed at page bottoms) are kept in annotations.jsonl, not merged into article text, because they are not part of the enacted article.
5. The Arabic version of the same code is not machine-readable: its PDF has a broken ToUnicode map and extracts as glyph names, not Arabic text. See excluded_sources in manifest.json.
6. Article text is re-flowed for readability: hard line breaks are joined, hyphenated word wraps are repaired, and enumerated items keep their own line. Wording, punctuation and article numbering are unchanged, but the line breaks do not match the printed page.
7. Heading titles are reproduced as published, including upper-case typography, and renumbered words: the published 'LIVRE PREMIER' is recorded as 'Livre I' so book labels sort consistently.
